## **Where to Find CA Firms & Finance Teams**

### **LinkedIn (Best for cold outreach)**
1. Search: "Chartered Accountant" + [City]
2. Filter: Open to work / Recently active
3. Connect with personal message (Include your pitch)

**Search strings:**
- "Chartered Accountant" + "Delhi"
- "Finance Manager" + "India"
- "CA Firm" + "Mumbai"
- "Account Manager" + "Bangalore"

### **Email Databases**
- **Apollo.io** (₹50/month) - Find CA firm emails
- **Hunter.io** (Free tier) - Extract emails from company websites
- **Clearbit** (Free tier) - B2B email finder

### **Direct Directories**
- **ICAI Directory** - https://www.icai.org (Search member directory)
- **JustDial** - Search "accounting firms near me"
- **LocalBusiness Pages** - Google Maps for "CA firms", "accounting services"

### **Business Communities**
- **LinkedIn Groups** - "CA Forum", "Finance Professionals India"
- **Facebook Groups** - Search for CA groups in your city
- **WhatsApp Groups** - Local business networking groups

## **Phone Script (For Warm Calls)**

**After sending email, wait 3 days → Call them**

```
Hi [Name], 

This is [Your Name]. I sent you an email about a tool we built for month-end close automation.

Did you get a chance to see it?

[Listen - they'll either say yes or no]

OBJECTION 1: "Not interested" / "Sounds complicated"
→ Response: "I get that. It's actually super simple - takes 2 minutes. No learning curve. 
Can I send you a 3-minute video demo instead? No pressure."

OBJECTION 2: "We're happy with our current process"
→ Response: "Makes sense. Quick question though - how many hours does your team spend on 
month-end close right now? Because most firms we talk to are stuck at 15-20 hours."

OBJECTION 3: "Can't afford it right now"
→ Response: "Totally fair. We have a free 30-day trial with no credit card. 
How about you try it this month and let me know if it saves you time?
If yes, you consider it. If no, zero commitment."

CLOSING: "How does a free trial sound? I'll send you the link. Takes 5 minutes to set up."

```

## **Quick Response Tips**

### ✅ Response Time Matters
- Reply to inquiries within 1 hour
- Be enthusiastic and helpful (not salesy)
- If they ask a question, answer it directly

### ✅ Social Proof
- Share customer results: "Saved X hours", "Caught Y errors", "Reduced audit findings by Z%"
- Get testimonials from first few customers
- Share before/after examples

### ✅ Handle Objections Gracefully
```
OBJECTION: "It's too expensive"
RESPONSE: "I get that. Most firms save 20-30 hours/month with it, 
which is ₹3,000-5,000 in labor savings. So it pays for itself in 2-3 weeks."

OBJECTION: "We don't use [specific software]"
RESPONSE: "No worries - it works with any GL export, including [software name]. 
Just export to CSV and upload."

OBJECTION: "I'll think about it"
RESPONSE: "Totally cool. How about this - try the free demo for a week and 
let me know if it's useful? Then you can decide."
```

## **First Customer Playbook**

Once someone says yes:

1. **Send them the link** → Let them upload sample GL
2. **Follow up in 3 days:** "How's it going? Any questions?"
3. **Share success:** Ask them to share a screenshot of their trial balance
4. **Convert to paying:** "Ready to move to paid plan?"
5. **Get testimonial:** "Would you be willing to share a quick testimonial?"
